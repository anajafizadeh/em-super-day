# Electric Mind Super Day Challenge

Build a dashboard that helps someone understand their investments: what they own, how much it is all worth, and how that has changed over time.

Choose your assigned track. Each track has the full requirements, a short setup guide, and sample data or a mock service to help you get started.

| Track    | Start here                      | Requirements                      |
| -------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Frontend | [Setup](frontend/START-HERE.md) | [Tasks](frontend/REQUIREMENTS.md) |
| Backend  | [Setup](backend/START-HERE.md)  | [Tasks](backend/REQUIREMENTS.md)  |
| Mobile   | [Setup](mobile/START-HERE.md)   | [Tasks](mobile/REQUIREMENTS.md)   |

You may choose your language and framework within your track's requirements. You may use AI assistance. Build your solution in your track's `solution/` folder. You do not need to complete the other tracks.

Read the requirements before coding. They describe what to build and how to check it. The supplied mocks are starting materials; the app or backend is yours to build. All data is fictional.

Include short notes with your solution: how to install and run it, how to run your tests, and any assumptions or unfinished work. Follow your interviewer's instructions on the time available and how to submit.
